# Brand Guide — Revelation Youth

> Defines the brand identity, voice, and creative direction.
> For concrete design tokens (hex values, spacing scale, type scale), see [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](./DESIGN_SYSTEM.md).

---

## 1. Brand Essence

Revelation Youth is the youth ministry of International Miracle Makers Church. The brand is **reverent yet youthful, editorial yet warm**. It should feel like a quiet, beautiful invitation — *Just a Moment With God* — not a marketing push.

**Tagline:** Just a Moment With God

---

## 2. Brand Personality

The website **should feel:**

- Premium
- Church-centered
- Editorial
- Artistic
- Youthful
- Clean
- Reverent
- Modern
- Calm
- Elegant
- Spiritually reflective
- Professional
- Welcoming

The website **should NOT feel:**

- Generic
- Game-like
- Neon
- Dark fantasy
- SaaS-like
- Childish
- Overbuilt
- AI-generated
- Like a nightclub
- Like a random event poster
- Like a basic church template

When a design choice is ambiguous, choose the option that is **calmer, more spacious, and more editorial**.

---

## 3. Visual Inspiration

Use these as references for **polish, spacing, interaction quality, and editorial composition** — never copy them directly:

- Elevation Rhythm
- Elevation Worship
- Hillsong
- Editorial magazine layouts
- Make Me Pulse-style creative interaction
- The Editorial Magazine-style composition

The goal is *their level of intentionality*, expressed through the Revelation Youth palette and voice.

---

## 4. Color Mood

The palette should evoke: **nature, reverence, luxury, modern church branding, calmness, spiritual reflection, editorial elegance, youthful beauty, peaceful energy.**

- **Backgrounds:** soft white / soft ivory dominate the site.
- **Primary accents:** forest green, moss green, olive green, muted teal, sky blue.
- **Secondary accents:** champagne gold, charcoal gray, stone gray.
- **Supporting tones:** dark green, dark gray-green, yellow green, gray green, gray azure.

**Rules:**

- White and soft ivory are the dominant backgrounds.
- Forest green and charcoal gray carry readable text.
- Champagne gold is used **sparingly** — accents, thin lines, hover states, small emphasis.
- The green/blue/gold Revelation Youth artwork is a **subtle texture**, never a full-site background.
- No neon. No random colors. Do not overuse dark backgrounds.

Exact hex tokens are defined in [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](./DESIGN_SYSTEM.md).

---

## 5. Typography Mood

Preferred display fonts (if available/licensed): **Peranory Condensed**, **Peranory Semicondensed**, **Amoresa**. If unavailable, substitute an elegant editorial serif/condensed display face and a clean readable sans for body (see DESIGN_SYSTEM fallbacks).

Typography should feel **editorial, elegant, premium, artistic, readable.**

- **Expressive type** for: brand title, page titles, event category titles, music titles, merch editorial headings.
- **Clean readable type** for: body, metadata, captions, footer, form labels.

Do not make all text decorative. Brand moments can be expressive; body content must stay readable.

---

## 6. Imagery & Texture

The Revelation Youth artwork is used as a **subtle hero texture, brand accent, section accent, and recurring motif** — never a full-page dark wash.

Imagery should feel **natural, warm, church-centered, editorial, premium.**

Avoid: fake stock-photo feel, AI-generated faces, harsh filters, overprocessed visuals, random decorative blobs, generic AI gradients.

---

## 7. Voice & Tone (Copywriting)

- Warm, sincere, unhurried. Speak like a welcoming friend, not a brand.
- Short, clear sentences. Reverent without being preachy.
- No hype, no exclamation overload, no SaaS marketing speak ("Supercharge your faith!").
- Never invent spiritual claims, testimonies, or quotes.
- When content is missing, say so plainly via placeholder (see [CONTENT_PLAN.md](./CONTENT_PLAN.md)).

---

## 8. Logo & Wordmark Usage

- The primary brand expression is the **Revelation Youth wordmark** set in the display typeface.
- Give the wordmark generous clear space.
- Pair with the tagline *Just a Moment With God* in a smaller, calmer weight.
- Do not stretch, recolor with neon, or place over busy imagery without sufficient contrast.

---

## 9. Brand Do / Don't Summary

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use white space as a feature | Cram cards to fill space |
| Use gold sparingly | Make gold a primary fill |
| Keep imagery warm + natural | Use AI faces or harsh filters |
| Let one idea lead each section | Overload the homepage |
| Keep motion subtle | Add parallax, glows, game motion |
| Mark missing content clearly | Invent events, lyrics, prices |
